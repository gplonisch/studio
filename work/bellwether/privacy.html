What the booking form collects

A name, an email address, a date, a party size, and anything you write in the notes box. The notes box is where allergies go, which means it can contain health information, so it is treated more carefully than the rest: it is read by the kitchen, used for your booking, and not kept afterwards.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising pixels. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Third parties
